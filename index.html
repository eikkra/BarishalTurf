    Before you book

    How does booking work?

    Pick a day, a start time and how long you want to play, then send the request. We hold that time for hours while you send the advance, then confirm on WhatsApp.

    How much advance do I pay?

    % of the total, by bKash, Nagad or cash at the counter. The rest is paid before kick-off.

    Can I change or cancel?

    Call or WhatsApp us as early as you can. We move bookings to any free hour.

    Which boots can I wear?

    Turf shoes or rubber studs. Metal studs damage the turf and are not allowed.
