      Before you book

      How does booking work?

      Pick a day, tap the hours you want and send the request. We hold those hours for hours while you send the advance, then confirm on WhatsApp.

      How much advance do I pay?

      At least % of the total, by bKash, Nagad or cash at the counter. The rest is paid before kick-off.

      Can I book hours that are not next to each other?

      Yes. Tap any free hours on the same day, for example 6–7 PM and 9–10 PM. Each block gets its own booking ID.

      Can I change or cancel?

      Call or WhatsApp us as early as you can. We move bookings to any free hour.

      Which boots can I wear?

      Turf shoes or rubber studs. Metal studs damage the turf and are not allowed.
